About Us

Healthcare That Comes to Your Door

Doctor At Home – Delhi NCR is a home healthcare service that connects families with qualified doctors for consultations at home.

Who we are

Falling ill rarely happens at a convenient time. Travelling across a busy city to a clinic, waiting in line and travelling back can be exhausting for a patient – and nearly impossible for an elderly parent or a young child with a fever.

Doctor At Home exists to make that first consultation easier. You tell us what you need and where you are; our team checks availability and arranges for a qualified MBBS/MD doctor to visit your home across Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Faridabad.

We are not an emergency service. For life-threatening situations, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital. For everything else – fevers, infections, routine reviews, elder care, injections and sample collection – a home visit can be a calm and practical choice.

What we stand for

Our approach to home healthcare

  • Care where you are

    Healthcare should reach families who find travel hard – elders, children and patients who are unwell.

  • Qualified doctors

    Consultations are provided by MBBS/MD doctors. We publish only credentials that have been supplied and verified.

  • Straightforward information

    A clear starting price of ₹699, honest availability and no unsupported promises.

  • Respect and privacy

    Patients and families are treated with dignity, and personal details are used only to arrange the visit.
